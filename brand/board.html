The mark

The letter A, in braille
A braille cell has six dot positions. The letter A is a single raised dot in the top left. That is our mark.
One sure point
The solid dot is the one thing to do now. The rings are everything else, waiting their turn.
Built for touch and small screens
Round, even shapes that stay clear on a 16 px tab. At small sizes the rings become small dots.
Clear space: one dot on every side
