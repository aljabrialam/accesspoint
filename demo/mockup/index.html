AccessPoint: design mockup

Static mockup of the phone app, one video scene, and the design tokens. Every machine shown is a drawing of a demo kiosk we build ourselves, not a real bank. Nothing here is wired to the models yet.

App screens

One large step at a time, spoken aloud and shown in big type. The yellow comes from tactile paving and marks the one thing to act on now. Green and red appear only for outcomes.

Demo video scene

The demo layout from the hackathon-demo-video skill: phone on the left, demo count, title, quoted input, status and the six-step track on the right, one caption at a time.

Tokens

Type is the system font (SF Pro on iPhone) so it follows Dynamic Type in the app. Steps are 29 pt bold; nothing a user must read is below 15 pt.
