## Description

```
Stuck at a machine? Send a photo. Get the next step.

AccessPoint helps older people, blind and low-vision people, and migrants get through ticket machines, check-in kiosks and self-checkouts safely. Send a photo of the screen in a chat and get one step at a time, in your own language, as text and a voice note. It stops you when something looks wrong, and it never needs your PIN.

Built for the Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon 2026 (Indie Developer track, Build for Your World) with Meta's Muse Spark, SAM 3.1, Muse Voice Transcribe and Muse Image.

Try it in your browser: https://accesspoint-bot.aljabrialam.workers.dev/try
Telegram (live): https://t.me/accesspointhelpbot
Source code: https://github.com/aljabrialam/accesspoint

How it works
Muse Spark reads the photo and plans one step as strict JSON. SAM 3.1 outlines every button, and code points only where SAM's outline and Muse Spark's reading agree. Muse Voice Transcribe understands spoken answers in 25 languages. Code, not the AI, makes every safety decision.

Tested on the real models (3 Nov 2026): Muse Spark 7/7 screens, Muse Spark + SAM 3.1 5/5 right buttons, Muse Voice 3/3 spoken answers, 20/20 bot tests, 11/11 app flows.

Honesty notes: the iPhone app runs scripted scenarios on demo kiosks we built. Telegram and the web page are live. Messenger and WhatsApp are code complete and tested; their scenes replay real bot replies and go live after Meta's app review. Narration drafted by Muse Spark and voiced with Microsoft Edge neural voices.
```
